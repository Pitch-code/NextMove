## Full description (max 4000)
```
Scam messages are getting harder to spot. NextMove quietly checks new SMS, WhatsApp and other chat notifications on your phone and warns you when a message shows common fraud warning signs, then tells you exactly what to do next.

WHAT NEXTMOVE DOES

• Scam message alerts: checks new message notifications on your phone for warning signs such as fake KYC updates, "account blocked" threats, OTP or UPI PIN requests, digital-arrest calls, fake refunds, lottery wins and work-from-home task scams.
• Clear next steps: every alert explains why the message looks risky and what to do, with one-tap access to India's 1930 cyber-fraud helpline and the cybercrime.gov.in reporting portal.
• Number and link checker: paste a suspicious phone number or link to check it for scam patterns such as shortened links, look-alike bank websites and unusual numbers.
• "I've been scammed" emergency steps: a calm, step-by-step guide for the first minutes after a fraud.
• Trusted contact: save a son, daughter or friend and alert them in one tap when something looks wrong.
• Voice check: describe a call or message in your own words and get an instant safety check.
• Reminders: set reminders for bills, appointments and return deadlines, with the details that matter (amount, place, order number).
• Scam of the week: short tips on the latest scams in India.
• Weekly safety summary of how many messages were checked and flagged.

MADE FOR INDIA

• English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Bengali.
• Large text, high-contrast and dark mode options for easier reading.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• Messages are checked on your phone. NextMove does not upload your messages and has no account or login.
• NextMove does not ask for SMS, call-log or contacts permissions. Message alerts use Android's Notification Access, which you can turn off at any time.
• It cannot read your past inbox, only new message notifications.

FREE TRIAL AND PREMIUM

• All features are free for 7 days, with ads.
• Premium is a one-time payment of ₹499 through Google Play. No subscription, no renewals. Premium removes ads and keeps every feature unlocked.

PLEASE NOTE

NextMove gives automated warnings based on common scam patterns. It cannot catch every scam and may sometimes flag a genuine message, so always verify through the official app, website or phone number of the organisation. NextMove is an independent app and is not affiliated with the Government of India, the 1930 helpline, any bank or any payment app.

Questions or feedback: veerar77@gmail.com
```
